About

Welcome to the Safety and Collective Intelligence (SCI) Autonomy Lab. SCI Autonomy Lab is a research group at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, directed by Jason J. Choi.

We study the science of intelligence for autonomous systems, such as robots and aircraft, with an emphasis on safety guarantees and collective intelligence.

Put more simply, we investigate how autonomous systems can make safer decisions and coordinate with other agents more effectively.
