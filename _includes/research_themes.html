Learning Safety for Humanoid Whole-Body Control

Safe control of humanoid robots remains challenging due to their high-dimensional dynamics, contact-rich interactions, and sensitivity to disturbances. In our recent work, we propose a framework that learns an implicit safety-filtering mechanism for humanoid control, using residual reinforcement learning.

Data-driven & Learning-enabled Safety Frameworks

Modular safety assurance mechanisms that use trajectory data to construct and update safety constraints with provable robustness.

Control Theory for Safety

Classical control-theoretic analysis provides safety assurance when mathematical assumptions and models hold. We extend these tools to disturbances, uncertain agents, and measurement errors.
