SCI Autonomy Lab @ UCLA

About

Welcome to the Safety and Collective Intelligence (SCI) Autonomy Lab.

SCI Autonomy Lab is a research group at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, directed by Jason J. Choi.

We develop the foundations of safe robotics and scale them to enable reliable collective autonomy. More broadly, we study the science of intelligence for embodied autonomous systems.

Control Theory

We study the mathematical science of decision-making in dynamical systems, with a primary focus on safety guarantees and multi-agent decision-making. Core concepts we investigate include nonlinear and hybrid systems theory, reachability analysis, optimal and predictive control, and game theory.

Machine Learning & Data Science

Machine learning provides methods that are generalizable across diverse applications, embodiments, and tasks. Our research focuses on advancing rigorous safety guarantees and principled multi-agent coordination for machine learning-based autonomy.

Robotics

Building on foundations in control-theoretic analysis and data-driven frameworks, we aim to advance robot intelligence with an emphasis on enabling robots to perform complex tasks that go beyond individual capabilities, tasks made possible only through well-coordinated teamwork.

Advanced Air Mobility

Innovations in aviation autonomy are enabling new operations such as air taxis and autonomous delivery, while introducing challenges in high-density traffic, uncertain demand, complex environments, and weather. Our research develops robust autonomous air mobility architectures by integrating vehicle-level intelligence with system-level operational decision-making.
